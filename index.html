About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Changhee Jung. My research focuses on computer architecture, with an emphasis on designing high-performance and energy-efficient CPUs and GPUs, Large Language Model (LLM) acceleration, compiler-architecture co-design, and cache hierarchies.
